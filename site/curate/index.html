    How Kleros Curate works for real
    1. Apps need clean lists: real tokens, real websites, correct labels.
    2. On Kleros Curate, anyone can add an entry by putting down a deposit.
    3. For a few days, anyone can challenge an entry that breaks the list’s policy.
    4. No challenge? It’s listed, and the submitter gets the deposit back.
    5. Challenged? Randomly drawn Kleros jurors decide. The winner takes the loser’s deposit, minus court fees. For a challenger, that’s the bounty.
    6. Wallets and block explorers show these lists to their users.

    Fun fact: Curate’s first live test, in 2018, was a list of doge pictures: Doges on Trial.
